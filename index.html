Python Hackathon 2025 is a one-day competitive event where students will develop innovative projects using Python frameworks and GUI libraries. Participants will work under a strict deadline to design, develop, and present their projects before a panel of judges.

RULES AND REGULATIONS

Competition Rules & Guidelines

General Rules

    Eligibility:
  • Open to all I, II, and III-year students from all departments.

    • Team Composition:
  • Each team will consist of 2 members.
  • Initially, each section has 4 teams, but one team per section will proceed further.

Event Structure

  • Problem Statements: Will be disclosed at the start of each round.
  • Technology Choice: Participants can use Python or Java.
  • Teams must select one problem statement per round (Level 2 & Level 3 only).

Competition Structure & Guidelines

    Level 1:
  • Easy Bugs (5-10 mins each) – 6 questions
  • Medium Bugs (10-15 mins each) – 6 questions
  • Problem-Solving with Edge Cases – 5 questions

    • Level 2:
  • Harder problems – 4 questions
  • Scenario-Based Coding

    • Level 3:
  • Case Study + Code Implementation

General Judging Criteria

    Level 1 (50 Points)
  • Number of Bugs Fixed – 20 points
  • Correctness & Code Readability – 20 points
  • Time Taken – 5 points

    • Level 2 (100 Points)
  • Functionality & Accuracy – 40 points
  • Logical Approach – 30 points
  • Efficiency & Optimization – 20 points
  • Presentation – 10 points

    • Level 3 (100 Points)
  • Problem Understanding & Analysis – 20 points
  • Code Implementation & Execution – 40 points
  • Efficiency & Edge Case Handling – 20 points
  • Innovation & Optimization – 10 points
  • Presentation & Justification – 10 points

Disqualification Rules

  • Code Plagiarism or Unauthorized Assistance
  • Violation of Time Limits
  • Tampering with Competition Platforms
  • Disruptive or Unethical Behavior
  • Multiple Submissions for the Same Problem
  • Non-Adherence to the Competition Theme

Prize Allocation Criteria

  • 🏆 1st Place - Best Overall Performance (Trophy + Certificate)
  • 🥈 2nd Place - Second Highest Cumulative Score (Trophy + Certificate)
  • 🥉 3rd Place - Best Debugging Team (Trophy + Certificate)
  • 💡 Best Debugger (Trophy + Certificate)
  • 🚀 Best Scenario-Based Solution (Trophy + Certificate)
